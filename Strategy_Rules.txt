PATRICK WIELAND'S 15-MINUTE OPENING RANGE STRATEGY
The exact rules we coded, in plain English. Coded and backtested by Backtest the Hype.
(The strategy idea belongs to Patrick Wieland. Not financial advice.)

=====================================================================
HIS RULES (from his video)
=====================================================================
1. THE BOX
   At 9:30 New York time the market opens. Mark the HIGH and the LOW of the
   first 15-minute candle (9:30 to 9:45). That is the opening range.

2. DO NOTHING INSIDE THE BOX
   No trade until that 15-minute candle has closed, and no trade while price
   is still inside the range.

3. THE BREAKOUT DECIDES THE DAY
   Price breaks ABOVE the high  -> only look for LONGS today.
   Price breaks BELOW the low   -> only look for SHORTS today.

4. ENTRY
   Enter in the breakout direction on the lower timeframe (1 to 5-minute chart).

5. STOP LOSS
   On the other end of the candle you enter on:
   longs = that candle's low, shorts = that candle's high.
   Size the position so this stop = your fixed risk.

6. NO FIXED TARGET
   Trail the stop behind each new higher low (longs) or lower high (shorts)
   and let the move run until the stop is hit.

7. NEWS
   If economic numbers come out at 9:45 or 10:00, wait for them first.

=====================================================================
OUR DEFINITIONS (how we made it exact - NOT his words, all are settings)
=====================================================================
- Breakout: a 1-minute candle CLOSES outside the box. A wick doesn't count.
- Fresh break only: the candle before closed inside the box, this one closes outside.
- Entry: at the open of the next candle, at the real bid/ask.
- Stop: the breakout candle's opposite extreme, no buffer.
- Trailing stop: each new confirmed swing (2 candles on each side; ties don't
  count). The stop only ever moves in the trade's favour.
- Direction for the day: locked by the first break after the box closes.
- Entry window: 9:45 to 12:00 New York. Every trade closed by 15:55 New York.
- One trade at a time, max 1 trade per day. A re-entry needs price to close
  back inside the box first.
- News: tested as "earliest entry = 10:00" (the MT5 news calendar does not work
  in the Strategy Tester). This is our delay test, not his exact news rule.
- 1R = the amount lost if the first stop is hit.

=====================================================================
THE 7 VERSIONS WE TESTED (each changes ONE thing from V1)
=====================================================================
V1  His rules (trail, breakout-candle stop, 1-minute close, from 9:45)
V2  Fixed 2R target
V3  Fixed 3R target
V4  Trail, capped at 2R
V5  Classic ORB stop (other side of the box)
V6  Breakout confirmed on a 5-minute candle close
V7  Wait until 10:00 every day

=====================================================================
THE TEST
=====================================================================
Markets: NAS100, US500 (S&P 500), US30 (Dow) - Fusion Markets CFDs
Data: real ticks, January 2021 to October 2026
Costs: real spread + commission
Risk: $1,000 per trade = 1R
Size: 21 backtests, 28,703 simulated trades

HIS EXACT RULES (V1):  NAS100 +17.6R | US500 +26.9R | US30 +85.7R
All three together: +130.2R (+130% at 1% risk per trade, no compounding)

Best result:   V1 on the Dow, +85.7R
Best balance:  V6 (5-minute close) on the Dow, +75.8R, max drawdown 28.7R
Smoothest:     V5 (range stop) on the Dow, max drawdown 14.2R

THE 3 MISTAKES THAT BREAK IT
1. Taking profit early - fixed targets lost 80-90R on the Nasdaq.
2. Waiting for the open to settle - the 10:00 delay was the worst version on
   every market (-229.1R combined).
3. Skipping days - on the Nasdaq, his 5 best days made +73.5R. Without them the
   other 1,384 trades lost 55.9R. You can't predict which mornings those are.

PROP FIRM CHECK (1% risk, pass +8%, fail -10%, restart each time)
NAS100 his rules: 23 passes, 24 fails - close to a coin flip.
US30 range stop: 3 passes, 0 fails - but about 515 days per pass.
